Sales Performance Dashboard

An Excel-based sales analytics dashboard designed to monitor overall sales performance, regional performance, salesperson performance, product sales, monthly trends, and key business KPIs.

Dashboard Preview

Project Overview

This dashboard transforms raw sales data into a clear management report. It provides a quick overview of sales performance through KPI cards, regional analysis, salesperson performance, product analysis, and monthly sales trends. Interactive Region and Product filters allow users to analyze the dashboard from different views.
